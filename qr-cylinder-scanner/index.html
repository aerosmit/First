Cylinder QR Scanner

Reads QR codes wrapped around small cylinders (≈1.6 cm vials/tubes) by digitally un-curving the label in real time.

Hold the cylinder upright and line its left/right edges up with the two green guide lines. Keep it 12–15 cm from the camera — closer than ~10 cm the iPhone cannot focus — and use the zoom slider (2–3×) to fill the frame. The bottom preview strip must show crisp, separated squares.

⚠️ Camera access requires HTTPS (or localhost). Serve this page over HTTPS.
